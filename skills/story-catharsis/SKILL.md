# Story catharsis

1. Name the exact frustration in the viewer's words. Identify what it would mean to resolve
   it from the affected person's point of view. The change might be a correction, a return,
   a decision, an acknowledgment or a concrete limit on the next demand.
2. Call `mcp__social-flow__story_catharsis_plan` with the actual situation and format. In
   Claude Code use `mcp__plugin_social-flow_social-flow__story_catharsis_plan`. Filter by
   the desired outcome or request a named pattern. With no filter the tool returns the
   full 15-type catalogue. Compare the candidates against the authority, evidence,
   relationships and choices the story already has.
3. Use the mechanism that resolves the frustration. Plant whatever makes the result possible
   and give the relevant person a reason to act. In the scene, let the audience see the
   intervention and who responds. In the next beat, show what now stops, returns or becomes
   possible. A file can change a judge's decision. A boundary is legible the next time the
   demand arrives. A short can use one reaction to show the change. A longer story can pay off
   smaller obstructions along the way and still answer its central question.
4. Scale the result to the harm. A private acknowledgment, refund or exit can carry an ending.
   If the story earns an unresolved ending or has no obstruction to resolve, pay the promise
   with the appropriate discovery or consequence. In factual work preserve the actual outcome
   and what each source establishes.

For a standalone request deliver the requested story prose. In a synopsis put the payoff
and response in the storyline. On a storyboard point `sequence.payoff` at the scene that
delivers it and write visible evidence in the shots there. If the episode uses a catalogued
cliché, follow the existing [cliché selection rules](../storyboard/references/cliche-use.md).
