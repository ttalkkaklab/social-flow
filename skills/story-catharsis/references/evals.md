# Evaluation examples

| Request | Expected behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Add a satisfying reversal when a manager claims an employee's work | Compare record reveal and a formal correction. Establish the record early and show a decision actually changing. |
| Give a low-key family short a satisfying ending | Compare a specific boundary with leaving. Show the next demand and the new response, preserving the intended family relationship. |
| End a factual episode about an unresolved refund dispute | Keep the documented status. The outcome can be a change the source establishes or a question honestly left open. |
