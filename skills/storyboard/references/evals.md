Use a fresh author with the installed skill and raw brief. A separate reviewer first
receives only ordered actions and speech; do not supply intended interpretation or an
answer key. Keep artifacts local under data/. No paid generation is implied.

## Delegated storyboard delivery

Input: ask an author agent to storyboard a new episode with two people, a handed-over cup
and a single room; do not separately ask for HTML or sheets.
Pass evidence: the user sees the scenario before detailed scene/shot writing; no unsolicited
approval pause. Character identities/shared sheets, canonical prop sheet and room sheet
exist before shot design. Final delivery links the bundled HTML and actual `--check` result.
The prop and location sections show the cup/room and shot links with missing media pending.
Repeat with an explicit scenario-approval request: the author waits before detailed work.
Repeat with an explicit text-only request: the author delivers text and applicable sheet
information without claiming a template check. A silent abstract scene declares why unused
sheet categories are not applicable. Existing sheets are read and reused without paid calls.

## Causal dialogue

Input: write a fictional family conflict in which a replacement must deceive a visitor.
Pass evidence: the visitor's knowledge, the reason to continue, the replacement's motive
and means of deception are available in actual events. The decisive reaction has a planned
visible or audible delivery. No one-line-per-shot default or generic audience addressees.

## Silent physical comedy

Input: write a wordless comedy around a borrowed object and two characters with competing wants.
Pass evidence: ownership and transfer are visible, the gag's setup precedes payoff, actions
fit the planned source/end states and the plan works without dialogue or a vocabulary score.

## Fair mystery

Input: write a mystery whose ending changes how an ordinary earlier detail is understood.
Pass evidence: the clue is perceptible before the reveal, the eventual explanation obeys
established rules and character knowledge, and synopsis-only information earns no credit.

## Duration pressure

Input: write a complete story on a vertical canvas with no stated duration; then ask for a
hard shorter cut that cannot preserve every event.
Pass evidence: first length derives from performance; the revision removes repetition,
simplifies or proposes a meaningful split rather than silently erasing causal setup. The
canvas does not manufacture a runtime target. Concurrent actions do not all add sequential time.

## Boundary

Input: produce a storyboard only, then review its visual quality.
Pass evidence: no media generation or publishing; staging is assessed as a plan and actual
rendered quality stays unverified. Metadata and checks are not presented as viewed footage.

## Continuous tray action

Input: plan a woman carrying tea to a doorway, hiding, then putting the tray on a desk.
Pass evidence: source openings precede the action; the hidden pose is reached on screen or
through an intentional editorial skip. Camera variants share actor/prop world state.
Two adjacent shots do not independently restart the tray set-down merely because speech ends.

## Legacy spatial plan

Input: inspect a board with generic opening states and one numeric camera reused for a wide
view and reverse view; omit continuityGroup and blocking.
Pass evidence: missing/inferred connections and conflicting camera candidates are visible
as warnings. No invented actor poses, fabricated visual pass or new approval step.

## Production boundary

Input: plan connected shots before any video exists, with a supplied planned ending image.
Pass evidence: the ending stays planned. Observed endpoints stay missing; no media is
generated or extracted by storyboard, and the production handoff explains how to capture
the accepted selected interval later.

## A newcomer who knows too much

Input: a visitor recognizes a worker, then announces the worker's secret role without
having heard the arrangement. Rewrite the scene without changing its intended discovery.
Pass evidence: recognition and further inference have distinct available cues; the visitor
has a reason to arrive and speak. The repair need not explain the entire relationship.

## Clear but inert comedy

Input: a comedy draft explains every connection but its jokes can be moved between speakers.
Pass evidence: different pursuits or methods generate the complication; the audience can
anticipate and follow its reversal. The review quotes comic events, not just clear exposition.
No joke count, mandatory punchline ending or universal three-beat formula.

## Revised opening with a stale ending

Input: a whole-episode rewrite with an improved opening, an unchanged legacy tail and old
review references. Ask for content only.
Pass evidence: the author reads and revisits the tail, preserves canon, fixes consequential
knowledge gaps, and refreshes addressees/references. New text has no inherited media approval.
A blind reviewer scores the entire new content using a pre-fixed content rubric; images and
audio stay unreviewed. Failed iterations lead to a reusable plugin change and an installed
skill reload before the author resumes, not a coordinator-written answer-key script.

## Costly agreement and foreseeable mishap

Input: a paid substitute knowingly takes a serious risk, then ignores a known visitor;
the synopsis alone explains the substitute's secret motive and the time being bought.
Pass evidence: the performance supplies a specific proportionate reason to accept and
the present benefit of the interval. The mishap follows a readable flaw, competing need
or disrupted precaution. No automatic poverty/trauma backstory or exhaustive security
lecture. Repeated comic refusals vary stakes or tactics rather than restating the same joke.

## Overexplained repair

Input: a revision now explains every link but announces its exact punchline and spends
several beats on routine agreement paperwork; the named goal feels like a checklist item.
Pass evidence: one economical behavior gives the goal personal weight, precautions retain
readable risk without automatically naming the coming joke, and routine completion is
compressed while contested terms and actual prop transfers survive. Deliberate suspense
remains valid; do not erase necessary context merely to surprise the audience.

## Clear goal with a decorative flaw

Input: write a scenario-only workplace drama about a mentor who cares for a junior but
fears becoming unnecessary. The initial draft has a clear promotion goal and calls the
mentor controlling, yet every action is uncomplicated help. Existing identities already
establish these tendencies. Ask for more character depth without a tragic backstory.
Pass evidence: the performed exchange makes the mentor's competing priorities affect a
choice and lets the junior respond from their own pursuit. The reviewer can cite the
choice and its cost without reading the premise. No invented childhood, extra identity
keys, media generation or mandatory redemption; scenario-only scope stays intact.
Changing only the flaw description does not count as a repair.

## A stable conviction and distinct relationships

Input: a principled repairer refuses to falsify a report, even at personal cost. Their
employer wants discretion; their sibling fears losing the family's income. Write the
scene without making the repairer abandon the principle or confess a secret.
Pass evidence: the stable conviction remains valid, but the repairer's tactics and
responses differ with each counterpart and the cost reaches the actual exchange. The
other characters can affect events. No forced self-deception, moral ambiguity or lesson.
The reviewer distinguishes depth revealed under pressure from personality transformation.

## Character evidence lost in shots

Input: a complete scene shows a mentor reaching for a remote, a junior keeping it and
the mentor withdrawing their hand. The board crops to a face throughout and starts with
the mentor already smiling; author notes claim that they relinquish control.
Pass evidence: the author preserves the trigger, contested remote and response in the
performance and planned framing, with an opening before the decisive action. Optional
acting notes support the same situation without adding a spoken explanation. The reviewer
cites the missing cue in the original plan and judges the revision as planning evidence
only; no generated image, voice or video quality is claimed.

## Serialized consequence and narrator boundary

Input: draft a sequel using a canonical prior ending in which a partner withdrew trust.
Also provide a later unapproved alternative in which they reconciled. Then request an
unrelated narrator-only factual explainer using the same channel.
Pass evidence: the sequel starts from canonical distrust and earns any restoration in
events; it does not silently promote the alternative or overwrite shared identity. The
source episode and relevant resulting state are recorded in storyboard.md when useful.
The explainer adds no invented personal conflict or therapeutic arc. Missing history is
reported as uncertain, not filled in as fact; no new required schema or approval gate.
