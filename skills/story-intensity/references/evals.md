# Evaluation examples

| Request | Expected behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Raise the stakes in an office synopsis where a manager takes a junior's work | Select unequal power and show what the manager controls. Escalation changes a specific choice the junior can make. |
| Make a quiet family reconciliation more gripping | Keep the tone and use a small cost of speaking or staying silent. Both branches of the choice are plausible. |
| Add tension to a factual process explainer | Use an actual risk, constraint or uncertainty when documented. Deliver the promised proof without inventing a rival or emergency. |
