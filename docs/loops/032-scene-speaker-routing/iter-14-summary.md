# Verify recorded render operations and conditioning recordings

When a proposed speaking clip shares frames with a locally identified completed render, attachment and assembly verify the original operation, selected model and soundtrack. This also covers earlier pictures used for off-screen narration and speechless cards. A genuinely new picture or a legitimate container change retains its original eligibility.

An A2V receipt's driver applies to an audible output as well as a silent one. The current line audio and timing are checked against that driver. An audible take supplies its own soundtrack during assembly. A silent take retains the original conditioning WAV. Tests cover imports through public attachment and archived bindings through real reel assembly.
