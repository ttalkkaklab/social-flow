# Check the episode's previously selected takes

The speech editor archives the board before each change. Attachment and assembly now verify those snapshots against their existing content hashes and recover superseded dialogue bindings. A known older take cannot accept a different soundtrack after another take has occupied its slot. Earlier paths also contribute generation receipts when the active clip has moved.

Current authored trims take precedence for the same bound performance. A genuine replacement or the original take with its original recording remains usable. The independent public A2V reproducer now rejects the overdub before saving.
