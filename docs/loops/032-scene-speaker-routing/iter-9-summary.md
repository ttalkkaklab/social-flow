# Enforce render capacity at speech attachment and assembly

Video planning, speech preparation and assembly use the same native-render capacity rule. Capability comes from the selected adapter and model. Speech preparation can check the authored grouping before images are available. A stored native binding must also fit the real render when an existing episode is assembled.

The acceptance cases include a three-voice MSR one-take rejected at planning, attachment and assembly, a scene whose existing cuts carry different voice pairs, a three-voice Seedance render, and a preconditioned soundtrack. The prior five-shot EP13 and stereo A2V assembly regressions remain part of the full suite.

A public native attachment also has to decode to a real video frame and audible audio. Corrupt bytes or an audio file renamed to MP4 cannot be marked current. An audible native video without an adapter receipt remains importable when its selected model and authored route agree.
