# Storyboard template audit against camera-planning practice

26 November 2026 · Checkout `66119936` · Installed Social Flow package `0.129.0`

The shot card is the appropriate home for framing, angle, camera movement, focus and the action a cut reveals. The strongest defects appear when the implementation gives two different decisions the same storage or inheritance rule. A director's scene-wide instruction can disappear precisely when the cinematographer fills in the individual shots.

The comparison uses the [120-source planning study](README.md). The shot-list hierarchy follows the [Shot Lister worked example](https://www.shotlister.com/post/ultimate-guide-how-to-build-a-shot-list) and [Celtx shot fields](https://support.celtx.com/hc/en-us/articles/218530327-Shot-Lists). The distinction between a scene rule and the cue for a particular move is visible in [Kim Ji-yong's account of Decision to Leave](https://cine21.com/news/view/?mag_id=100490). Rehearsing focus marks and timing is documented in the [focus puller occupational standard](https://www.ukstandards.org.uk/en/nos-finder/SKSC22/maintain-required-focus-and-camera-operation-throughout-shoots).

## Findings that affect the compiled direction

| Priority | Current behavior and counterexample | Where the decision belongs |
|---|---|---|
| High | **Scene camera policy competes with the default move.** Both scene fields passed their schemas. With `directionDefaults.camera = camera.static` and `videoDirection.camera = Move only after the seal breaks.`, the Korean settings panel showed a static camera. The condition appeared in neither the draft panel nor the locally compiled provider prompt. Removing the default restored the sentence for a shot inheriting camera direction. Explicitly assigning both shots their own movement lost it again. | Keep the scene's rule as its own sentence. Resolve an optional default movement and each shot's actual move separately. An explicit static shot is a valid choice. |
| High | **Attention is overwritten by focus or framing.** The scene instruction was to withhold the ring until the daughter looked up. Both shots had concrete opening subjects and focus targets. The scene settings still displayed the instruction, but the draft and compiled prompt contained only the local attention values. Removing both subject and depth on one card restored the scene instruction in that card's portion. A rack focus can also become the replacement automatically. | Narrative disclosure belongs to the scene. A shot identifies the opening subject, the focal plane and the time of a reveal. All can apply simultaneously. |
| High when timing determines meaning | **A focus handoff has endpoints but no dedicated trigger on the focus control.** On a generated-video shot the card tooltip can show the reason alongside its two subjects. Putting the latch-click instruction only in that reason produced a prompt with both subjects and no click cue. The same words in the shot's timeline action survived compilation. This does not require sending editorial reasons to a model. | Put the cue on the focus change inside the shot, or bind it to an existing action beat. Preserve the reason separately. A fixed camera with a focus pull is one shot. |

Code paths for these reproductions are the [two camera fields](../../../server/src/msr-opening.ts), [shared resolver](../../../skills/storyboard/references/scene-direction.js), [scene request compiler](../../../server/src/scene-video.ts), [shot focus control](../../../server/src/storyboard.ts) and [focus sentence builder](../../../server/src/video-prompt.ts).

**Spatial continuity has a related ambiguity.** In the fixture, the mother sat beside the window throughout the scene. A second-shot continuity note stated that the seal was open. In the shot details the latter replaced the room relationship, and the compiler put the original relationship in the first shot only. A model reading the full prompt still sees that first mention. The local note records a prop change, so it should be possible to add it while retaining the room constraint across both shots. A genuine change of position should have an explicit way to supersede the earlier state. [Continuity resolution](../../../skills/storyboard/references/scene-direction.js)

## Findings about the review page and field meanings

| Finding | Observed consequence | Proposed placement |
|---|---|---|
| **A planned scene has no direction panel until it has a shot.** The page builds its scene groups by iterating over shots. | Three authored scenes with shots only in the first two yielded two scene cards. The third scene's saved camera axis was absent from the page. A separate sound section can appear for a scene without shots. | Build scene sections from the declared scene order. Show the blocking, axis, light and camera plan as soon as the director writes them. Attach the shot cards as they arrive. |
| **A scene-wide design note sits inside its first shot.** The field is keyed by scene number. | The test note about keeping the family together appeared inside the first shot article. The other shot had no indication that the note governed it. | Put the note in the scene header or settings panel. Repeating a link from an individual shot is optional. |
| **Shot size includes viewpoint, cast arrangement and editorial function.** The size vocabulary accepts OTS, POV, two-shot, reaction and cutaway. OTS can also occur under angle or opening viewpoint. | Waist-up and tight close-up shots, both over the shoulder, displayed the same size icon. The individual crops were retrievable in shot details. Size summaries also read the single size value. The project's directing guide already recognizes the distinction. | Give the primary size display the actual crop. Show participant arrangement or viewpoint beside it. Keep the purpose of a reaction or cutaway independently identifiable. Read older cards using their existing crop where available. |
| **Sound perspective is nested under optical depth.** The depth contract also expects a count of readable items and an explanation for a shallow image with two of them. | Two faces equally distant from the camera required a reason for shallow depth with two readable subjects. Providing full-room sound on that image produced a mix warning. The card included that sound choice in its depth tooltip. | Show sharp planes, subjects and changes on the visual side. Record a continuing room perspective at scene level and a local listening change at shot or beat level. A scene can have an optional depth or lens intent without specifying a stop for every shot. |
| **A flat chart displays missing cinematography.** | An HTML data graph without a camera effect showed both movement and depth as unset, although the depth checker classified the route as inapplicable. | Omit those controls for a flat graphic. Its designed animation, if any, belongs with the graphic. Distinguish an applicable but undecided value from an inapplicable one. |
| **The scene's unsaved preview omits some authored shot attributes.** | The two shot cards showed eye-level angle and shallow depth. The scene's draft prompt panel included both crops but neither the angle nor the optical instruction. The compiled provider prompt contained both. A matching saved plan follows a separate display path. | Keep one request at scene level. Build its preview from the effective per-shot directions or clearly identify the preview as a partial outline. |

See the [review template](../../../skills/storyboard/references/storyboard-html-template.html), [size vocabulary and optical validation](../../../skills/storyboard/references/structure-contract.js), [shot-size summaries](../../../skills/storyboard/references/shot-direction.js) and [directing guide on distance and viewpoint](../../../skills/storyboard/references/directing-grammar.md).

**The source labels also deserve care.** A shot that explicitly repeated the scene's static move showed a shot override in Korean. The label currently records where the author supplied the value. It can imply a visible change even when the result is identical. Distinguish repeating the scene value from a material exception if the label is used to scan for exceptions. ## How to organize the existing information

| Level | What a reviewer should see |
|---|---|
| Scene | Narrative point of view and reveal, shared geography and axis, lighting motivation, camera or depth policy, and any recurring default. Set the scene opening once. |
| Shot | Effective size and viewpoint, composition and eyeline, opening and closing frame, move or deliberate hold, focus subjects, and changes to blocking or lighting. Show the source of an inherited value on inspection. |
| Within a shot | A linked action, line or time cues a reframe, a shift of focus or a change in performance. Several moments can share one shot card. |
| Production details | Keep one compiled video request and a continuous soundtrack at scene scope. The camera route, frame rate and provider metadata can live in production details. A specific focal length or aperture can be recorded when a shot or a tested production look needs it. |

## Verification scope

The installed template, scene resolver, structure contract and compiled scene/video prompt modules matched the corresponding checkout files byte for byte. A temporary board exported with the repository's storyboard HTML builder was opened in Ego Lite with Korean UI. It included two generated-video shots in one scene, a data graph in another and a third scene awaiting its first shot. The scene camera fields passed their respective schemas. The two opening views passed the shot-view validator. Controls that removed one field at a time distinguished precedence from missing data. The compiler probes used the shipped `prepareSceneRequest` path and inspected its returned `providerPrompt` locally. They establish what the board displays and the compiler writes. Actual model adherence, old episode exports and a remote gateway job were outside this audit.
